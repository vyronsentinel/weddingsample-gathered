Our story

How we got here

Four chapters, one love. Keep scrolling.

01
Chapter one

Where it began

It started the way the best things do: quietly, and without a plan. A first hello turned into a long walk, and the walk turned into a habit neither of us wanted to break.

Somewhere between the first laugh and the last light of the day, we realised we had stopped walking toward something and started walking with someone.

One path, two hearts.
02
Chapter two

The little joys

Love, we learned, lives in small things: shared jokes, spontaneous detours, and a twirl in the open air just because the day felt too good to stand still.

Every ordinary day became a little brighter with her in it, and every laugh a little louder with him beside her.

Laughter always takes the lead.
03
Chapter three

The question

There was a quiet moment, a bouquet of white blooms, and one very simple question. No grand speech was needed. We both already knew the answer.

Our hearts said yes long before our words did, and that kiss said the rest.

A moment that belongs only to us.
04
Chapter four

Forever begins

Now we are holding each other as the evening light fades, with nowhere else we need to be and everyone we love about to gather around us.

On the fourteenth of March, we would be honoured to begin the next chapter with you.

Come celebrate with us.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

Garden formal: think a midi or maxi dress, tailored jumpsuit, or a suit in a light fabric. Blush, sage, champagne, ivory, and soft taupe are suggested, but any garden-appropriate color is welcome. Choose shoes that are comfortable on grass.

May I bring a plus-one?

Please check your invitation for the names of the guests included in your party. If you have questions, reach out to Isabelle and Julian before replying.

Will the celebration be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony and parts of the celebration are planned for the garden. Bring a light layer in case the evening gets cool.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Please include any dietary requirements in the RSVP note so the couple can coordinate with the caterer.
